## Matrix

Four certified regression projects were compiled at `O0`, `O1`, `O2`, and
`O3`, with `-g0`, `-gline-tables-only`, and `-g`. Object and executable output
were covered. Executables were linked with both `lld-link` and NSLink and then
executed with their expected exit-code contract. The measured order was
randomized with seed 200.

## Decisions opened by P00

1. O0 is the lowest-latency level in every measured object corpus, but its
   fixed frontend/HIR cost is still material.
2. O1, O2, and O3 spend most of their additional time in LLVM optimization and
   object emission. Their contracts must be evaluated against runtime and size,
   not compilation latency alone.
3. OOP HIR construction rebuilds the same syntax-binding indexes in each
   per-function worker. Sharing those immutable indexes is the first measured
   frontend optimization candidate because it affects every optimization level.
4. Full debug information roughly doubles several optimized executable builds;
   optimization and debug-information costs must remain independently visible.
5. NSLink is consistently competitive with, and in this corpus generally
   faster than, the `lld-link` executable path. Linker work remains separately
   attributable rather than being charged to an optimization level.
